      How Bloemstraat works

      Bloemstraat is a small group of volunteers who have been putting on events in Amsterdam since 2020. We know the venues, the causes and how to handle the money. You bring the idea and host it. We help with everything else, and every event raises money for a group doing the work.

      From idea to event

      Six steps
      1. 01

        Tell us your idea

        Half an idea is fine. A film, a dinner, a talk, a quiz, a night of music, or something we haven’t tried yet.

      2. 02

        We talk it through

        We meet, ask who it’s for and what it’s about, and choose together which group the money will go to.

      3. 03

        We find the room

        We know venues across Amsterdam that like what we do, and we’ll find one that fits.

      4. 04

        We make it together

        The poster, the listing, the door and the money on the night. You host it, and we’re there with you.

      5. 05

        The money goes out

        What the event raises goes to the group. Artists and contributors are paid as fairly as we can.

      6. 06

        It can grow

        If it works, it can come back again and again, and become a series with its own name.

      Where your event can sit

      • A once-off, like Open Table: a film, a dinner and a market in one day.

      • Your collective and us, under both names, like RepresentAsian × Bloemstraat.

      • A series that keeps coming back, with its own look and its own crowd.

      Run an idea past us.

      Tell us as much or as little as you have. We’ll get back to you to talk it through.

        About Bloemstraat

        Bloemstraat is committed to freedom, resistance and liberation. We make small, open events in Amsterdam where anyone can walk in, learn something and back a cause.

        It began in August 2020, when friends in Amsterdam raffled off each other’s work to raise money for Beirut after the port explosion.

        Since 2024 it has put on film screenings, listening sessions, dinners, markets and quizzes, in four series. The events are small on purpose.

        Who it’s for:
        Anyone. Most events are pay what you can, and you don’t need to know the subject before you arrive.
        Where the money goes:
        Most events raise money for a group working on the cause the event is about. Each event names the group.
        How to take part:
        Come to an event, bring one of your own, or help pay for one.

        The community

        Environment

        We want to bring our values to everything we do, and that includes what it costs the planet. As we learn and grow, we’ll keep looking at the footprint of our events and of this website, and keep making it smaller.

        This website:
        It is built to be light: small pictures, only where they add something, no tracking, and no video playing on its own. You can turn pictures down or off in the accessibility settings, and the bottom of each page shows how much it weighs.
        At events:
        We’re working out what this means for our events, from printing and food to how people get there. We’ll share what we learn here.
        Doing enough:
        We’d rather do a few things well than a lot of things quickly. Small events and a small website, kept going for a long time.

        For funders

        Bloemstraat puts on small, open events that bring people together and raise money for groups doing the work.

        How it works:
        Each event is about one cause and raises money for a group working on it. Most are pay what you can, so no one is kept out.
        What funding pays for:
        Venue hire, fees for artists and speakers, and access, such as subtitles and step-free venues.
        Talk to us:
        Send us a message and we’ll send you a short summary of our work: past events, who came and what they raised.

        Accessibility

        We want everyone to be able to come, whatever you can pay and whatever you need to take part. If something would stop you, tell us and we’ll do what we can.

        Paying:
        Most events are pay what you can, and you decide what that is. If the cost is what’s stopping you, message us and come anyway. No one will ask why.
        Getting in:
        Each event page says what we know about the venue: step-free entry, lifts, seating and toilets. If we don’t know something yet, we say so.
        At the event:
        You can come and go as you need, and there’s no need to stay to the end. Films have subtitles where we can get them.
        Coming with someone:
        If you’re bringing a friend or carer who helps you, they’re welcome too. Let us know and we’ll keep a seat.
        Ask before you come:
        If you need something to take part, like a seat near the front or a quieter spot, tell us before the event and we’ll sort what we can.
        On this website:
        It works with a keyboard and a screen reader. In the accessibility settings, in the menu, you can change the text size, spacing, contrast, typeface, motion and pictures.
        Tell us:
        If something here or at an event doesn’t work for you, message us and we’ll fix what we can.
